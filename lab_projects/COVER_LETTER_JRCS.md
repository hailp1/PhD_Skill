### Background and Scientific Contribution
The rapid acceleration of artificial intelligence (AI) across digital commerce platforms has transformed the retail interface from passive search directories into proactive, predictive discovery engines. While extensive technical research addresses recommendation accuracy, the cognitive-affective consumer dynamics governing AI-mediated impulsive purchasing remain fragmented.

To address this gap, our study adheres rigorously to the **PRISMA 2020 statement**, systematically synthesizing **1,441 peer-reviewed publications** from Scopus, Web of Science, and Dimensions, accompanied by a quantitative bibliometric network analysis of 866 core publications using VOSviewer.

### Key Theoretical Contributions
1. **Resolution of the Personalization Paradox**: Demonstrates how algorithmic accuracy and novelty operate as environmental stimuli (*S*) that reduce cognitive effort.
2. **The Mediating Role of Cumulative E-Satisfaction**: Formulates cumulative e-satisfaction as the indispensable dual cognitive-affective organism state (*O*) that disarms consumer vigilance and triggers impulsive urges.
3. **Boundary Condition of Ego Depletion**: Bridges retail marketing with Baumeister's self-control strength model, identifying acute executive fatigue and cognitive load as critical psychological catalysts that convert impulsive urges into immediate checkout behavior.
4. **Actionable 5-Pillar Agenda**: Outlines empirical pathways including fsQCA/NCA triangulation, neuro-marketing, and ethical guardrails against algorithmic dark patterns.
