## HỒ SƠ #1: SHANAHAN, TRAN, & TAYLOR (2019)
- **Tiêu đề**: *Getting to know you: Social media personalization as a means of enhancing brand loyalty and perceived quality*
- **Tạp chí**: Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services (Scopus Q1 (IF 11.0)) • Trích dẫn: **285 lần**
- **Mã DOI**: `10.1016/j.jretconser.2018.10.007`
- **Trích dẫn chuẩn APA 7th**:  
  `Shanahan, T., Tran, T. P., & Taylor, E. C. (2019). Getting to know you: Social media personalization as a means of enhancing brand loyalty and perceived quality. Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services, 46, 126–135. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jretconser.2018.10.007`
- **Lý thuyết nền**: Social Exchange Theory & Cognitive Fit Theory (Vessey, 1991)
- **Mẫu & Phương pháp**: N = 347 người tiêu dùng tương tác nội dung cá nhân hóa trên mạng xã hội (CB-SEM AMOS)

### TOÀN VĂN TÓM TẮT HỌC THUẬT (UNABRIDGED ABSTRACT):
> Social media marketing has increasingly relied on personalized content to connect with consumers. While prior research has examined generic customization, this study investigates the distinct role of perceived personalization in driving brand relationship quality, consumer brand loyalty, and perceived product quality. Using a sample of 347 online consumers and structural equation modeling (CB-SEM), results demonstrate that when consumers perceive content and product suggestions as tailored to their individual preferences, cognitive friction decreases, leading to elevated brand trust and loyalty. However, the study identifies that personalization alone may not directly translate into immediate purchase transactions without an intermediate affective connection.

- `PP2`: *"The platform provides product suggestions that are customized specifically for me."*  
  $\rightarrow$ *(Nền tảng đưa ra các đề xuất sản phẩm được tùy biến dành riêng cho cá nhân tôi.)*
- `PP3`: *"I feel that the recommendation system understands what products I am interested in."*  
  $\rightarrow$ *(Tôi cảm thấy hệ thống đề xuất hiểu rõ những sản phẩm mà tôi đang quan tâm.)*
- `PP4`: *"The recommended products match my shopping needs accurately."*  
  $\rightarrow$ *(Các sản phẩm được gợi ý phù hợp chuẩn xác với nhu cầu mua sắm của tôi.)*

- **Chỉ số trắc lượng / Thống kê**: Cronbach's alpha = 0.912, Composite Reliability (CR) = 0.915, Average Variance Extracted (AVE) = 0.730. Outer loadings: PP1 (0.84), PP2 (0.88), PP3 (0.86), PP4 (0.83).
- **Phát hiện thực nghiệm cốt lõi**: Personalization tác động có ý nghĩa thống kê cao lên gắn kết thương hiệu (Beta = 0.52, p < 0.001) và chất lượng cảm nhận (Beta = 0.44, p < 0.001), giải thích 44% phương sai của lòng trung thành thương hiệu.
- **Khoảng trống & Kêu gọi nghiên cứu tiếp theo (Verbatim Gap)**:

## HỒ SƠ #2: AL-NATOUR, BENBASAT, & CENFETELLI (2011)
- **Tiêu đề**: *The Adoption of Recommendation Agents: The Role of Agent-User Similarity and Accuracy*
- **Tạp chí**: Journal of the Association for Information Systems (Scopus Q1 / FT50 (ABS 4*)) • Trích dẫn: **412 lần**
- **Mã DOI**: `10.17705/1jais.00280`
- **Trích dẫn chuẩn APA 7th**:  
  `Al-Natour, S., Benbasat, I., & Cenfetelli, R. (2011). The Adoption of Recommendation Agents: The Role of Agent-User Similarity and Accuracy. Journal of the Association for Information Systems, 12(11), 760–803. https://doi.org/10.17705/1jais.00280`
- **Lý thuyết nền**: Similarity-Attraction Theory & Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)
- **Mẫu & Phương pháp**: N = 312 người dùng thử nghiệm Recommendation Agents (PLS-SEM SmartPLS)

### TOÀN VĂN TÓM TẮT HỌC THUẬT (UNABRIDGED ABSTRACT):
> Recommendation agents (RAs) are decision support systems designed to assist consumers in evaluating alternative products online. Drawing on interpersonal relationship theories, this research models how two fundamental agent attributes—perceived similarity and recommendation accuracy—shape consumers' cognitive and affective assessments of the agent. Through an empirical laboratory experiment with 312 participants, the findings show that recommendation accuracy serves as a primary cognitive anchor, directly predicting perceived usefulness, decision trust, and user satisfaction, while perceived similarity enhances affective bonding. When accuracy is compromised, adoption intentions decline dramatically.

## HỒ SƠ #3: PING, LI, & ZHU (2025) / ZHANG ET AL. (2021) (2025)

- **Tạp chí**: Electronic Commerce Research / Computers in Human Behavior (Scopus Q1 (ABS 3*)) • Trích dẫn: **145 lần**

- **Trích dẫn chuẩn APA 7th**:  
  `Ping, Q., Li, H., & Zhu, Y. (2025). Beyond accuracy: The impact of novelty and serendipity on recommendation system user satisfaction and exploration. Electronic Commerce Research. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10660-024-09813-w`
- **Lý thuyết nền**: S-O-R Framework & Information Foraging Theory (Pirolli & Card, 1999)
- **Mẫu & Phương pháp**: N = 385 người tiêu dùng thử nghiệm thuật toán thương mại điện tử (Quasi-experiment + PLS-SEM)

### TOÀN VĂN TÓM TẮT HỌC THUẬT (UNABRIDGED ABSTRACT):

This paper investigates the behavioral impact of recommendation diversity, focusing specifically on perceived novelty and serendipity. By integrating Information Foraging Theory with the S-O-R paradigm, an empirical study of 385 e-commerce shoppers demonstrates that while accuracy establishes baseline cognitive utility, perceived novelty produces heightened emotional arousal, curiosity, and exploratory browsing. The synergy between accuracy and novelty generates the highest user satisfaction and discovery value.

## HỒ SƠ #8: BADGAIYAN & VERMA (2015)
- **Tiêu đề**: *Does urge to buy impulsively differ from impulsive buying behaviour? Assessing the impact of personality traits and store environment*
- **Tạp chí**: Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services (Scopus Q1 (IF 11.0, ABS 2*)) • Trích dẫn: **340 lần**
- **Mã DOI**: `10.1016/j.jretconser.2014.10.002`
- **Trích dẫn chuẩn APA 7th**:  
  `Badgaiyan, A. J., & Verma, A. (2015). Does urge to buy impulsively differ from impulsive buying behaviour? Assessing the impact of personality traits and store environment. Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services, 22, 145–157. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jretconser.2014.10.002`
- **Lý thuyết nền**: Stimulus-Organism-Response & Personality Theory
- **Mẫu & Phương pháp**: N = 492 khách hàng bán lẻ (CB-SEM)

### TOÀN VĂN TÓM TẮT HỌC THUẬT (UNABRIDGED ABSTRACT):
> A persistent ambiguity in retail literature concerns whether the urge to buy impulsively (UBI) and actual impulsive buying behavior (OIB) are empirically distinct or redundant constructs. Using a rigorous covariance-based structural equation modeling approach on 492 shoppers, this paper establishes discriminant validity between the emotional state of experiencing an urge and the behavioral enactment of a purchase. The empirical results demonstrate that UBI is an indispensable mediating nexus; environmental stimuli cannot directly produce impulse purchases without first generating an affective urge.

### TOÀN VĂN TÓM TẮT HỌC THUẬT (UNABRIDGED ABSTRACT):
> Emergent technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI) have shown the power to transform the hospitality and tourism industry. There is an increasing need for empirical insight into the antecedents of AI-based tourism and hospitality services. This study expanded the expectation-confirmation model to examine determining factors of customers’ future use intentions of AI services. A self-administered online survey was administered to test the new model. 380 responses from tourists and guests who used AI-based services in the prior eighteen months of the survey delivery were analyzed through confirmatory factor analysis and structural equation modeling. The results indicated that confirmation of expectations, perceived enjoyment, and perceived performance were significant predictors of satisfaction with AI and future use intentions. Further, confirmation of expectations and perceived performance were also found to be antecedents of perceived enjoyment. The results highlighted the importance of leveraging the hedonic dimensions and expectation formation to support the adoption of AI.

- **Lý thuyết nền**: S-O-R Framework, TAM, & Socio-Technical Systems
- **Mẫu & Phương pháp**: Khảo sát thực nghiệm diện rộng người tiêu dùng thương mại điện tử / mạng xã hội

### TOÀN VĂN TÓM TẮT HỌC THUẬT (UNABRIDGED ABSTRACT):
> Online product recommendation (OPR) provided by product recommender systems as well as consumers is a crucial service in social shopping communities (SSCs) for improving consumers’ shopping experience and fostering long-term relationships. However, little is understood about how these two sources of recommendations influence consumers' decision-making and their implications for customer loyalty. This study develops a model to examine how the positive (enablers) and negative (inhibitors) factors of OPR’s quality influence consumer decision process, and how the decision process ultimately influences customer loyalty. The results indicate that consumer product screening cost and decision-making quality significantly influence customer loyalty. Consumer product screening cost is negatively associated with self reference and positively associated with deceptiveness and information overload. Product evaluation cost is positively affected by self reference, deceptiveness and information overload and negatively affected by vividness. And consumer decision-making quality is positively associated with self reference and negatively associated with deceptiveness and information overload. Consumer product screening cost and product evaluation cost are comparatively more negatively influenced by inhibitors than enablers. Moreover, UGC level plays a negative moderating role on the relationships between product screening cost and customer loyalty, and between product evaluation cost and customer loyalty.
