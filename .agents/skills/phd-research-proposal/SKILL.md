# PhD Research Proposal Development Skill (Marketing & Business Administration)

This skill guides the creation of a doctoral-level research proposal (Đề cương nghiên cứu luận án tiến sĩ) that meets international academic standards (Scopus/WoS Q1/Q2 standards) and Vietnamese Ministry of Education & Training (MOET) dissertation guidelines.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### 1. Identify and Articulate the Research Gap
A doctoral dissertation requires genuine novelty. Do not stop at a generic topic; pinpoint specific gaps:
- **Theoretical Gap**: Missing link between established theories, contradictory theoretical predictions, or untested boundary conditions.
- **Empirical/Evidence Gap**: Conflicting empirical findings in prior literature requiring clarification through mediator or moderator variables.
- **Contextual/Context Gap**: Transferring models to emerging markets, novel digital marketing environments (e.g., Generative AI in consumer journey, omnichannel, metaverse, green consumerism).
- **Methodological Gap**: Prior studies only used cross-sectional surveys; need for longitudinal, experimental (2x2 factorial), or mixed-methods.

### 2. Formulate Research Problem, Questions (RQs) & Objectives (ROs)
- **Central Research Problem**: Synthesize the practical tension and academic void.
- **Research Questions (RQs)**:
  - *RQ1 (Descriptive/Exploratory)*: What are the key dimensions/mechanisms of [Phenomenon] in [Context]?
  - *RQ2 (Explanatory/Relational)*: How and through what mechanisms does [Independent Variable] influence [Dependent Variable]?
  - *RQ3 (Boundary/Contingency)*: Under what conditions (moderators) does this relationship strengthen or attenuate?
  - *RQ4 (Managerial/Strategic)*: What actionable marketing strategies and managerial policies emerge from these empirical findings?
- **Research Objectives (ROs)**: Mirror RQs into actionable academic milestones (To explore..., To examine the mediating role of..., To assess boundary conditions...).

### 3. Select Grounding Theoretical Frameworks (Lý thuyết nền)
In Marketing & Business Administration, grounding theories provide explanatory power:
- **Consumer Behavior & Adoption**:
  - *S-O-R Model (Stimulus - Organism - Response)*: Ideal for digital, AI, and retail environments.
  - *TAM / UTAUT 1 & 2*: Technology adoption, AI marketing tools, fintech.
  - *Theory of Planned Behavior (TPB) / TRA*: Ethical consumerism, sustainable marketing, adoption intent.
  - *Cognitive Appraisal Theory*: Consumer emotional responses, service recovery, satisfaction/delight.
  - *Flow Theory & Customer Engagement Theory*: Gamification, immersive virtual experiences.
- **Strategic Management & Organizational Marketing**:
  - *Resource-Based View (RBV) & Dynamic Capabilities*: Marketing capabilities, firm performance, digital transformation.
  - *Service-Dominant (S-D) Logic*: Value co-creation, service ecosystems.
  - *Social Exchange Theory & Relational Capital*: B2B marketing, influencer marketing, brand communities.

Consult [theories_in_marketing.md](./references/theories_in_marketing.md) for full summaries and sample applications.

### 4. Build the Proposed Conceptual Model & Formulate Hypotheses
- Define:
  - **Exogenous / Independent Variables (IV)**: Direct antecedents.
  - **Endogenous / Dependent Variables (DV)**: Target outcomes (e.g., Continuous Purchase Intention, Brand Loyalty, Firm Performance).
  - **Mediator Variables (M)**: The underlying psychological or organizational mechanism ("Why/How it works").
  - **Moderator Variables (W/Z)**: Boundary conditions (e.g., Consumer AI literacy, Risk aversion, Generational cohorts).
  - **Control Variables**: Demographics, firm age/size, prior experience.
- **Hypothesis Formulation Standard**:
  - State directional hypotheses clearly ($H_1$, $H_2$, etc.).
  - Accompany every hypothesis with theoretical justification (minimum 2-3 paragraphs citing foundational literature).

### 5. Compile the PhD Proposal Document
Structure the document following [proposal_template.md](./resources/proposal_template.md):
1. Title & Executive Summary
2. Urgency & Background of the Study (Tính cấp thiết)
3. Research Gap & Problem Statement
4. Research Questions & Objectives
5. Literature Review & Theoretical Grounds
6. Conceptual Model & Hypotheses System
7. Methodology Overview (Sampling, Instrumentation, Analytical Strategy)
8. Expected Contributions (Theoretical & Practical)
9. Timeline, Feasibility & References (APA 7th)
