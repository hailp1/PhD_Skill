# PhD Systematic Literature Review (SLR) Skill (Marketing & Business Administration)

This skill directs the execution of high-rigor literature reviews, transforming descriptive article summaries into a critical, structured, and publishable academic review (e.g., following PRISMA guidelines and literature matrices).

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### 1. Define Protocol and Eligibility Criteria
Establish formal inclusion and exclusion criteria before querying databases:
- **Inclusion Criteria**:
  - Peer-reviewed articles in ABS/AJG (rank 2, 3, 4, 4*) or ABDC (rank B, A, A*) or Scopus Q1/Q2.
  - Published within target time window (e.g., last 5–10 years for recent tech topics, or foundational classics).
  - Directly addressing the key constructs (e.g., AI in marketing, consumer decision journey).
  - Empirical studies (quantitative, qualitative, mixed) and high-impact conceptual reviews.
- **Exclusion Criteria**:
  - Conference extended abstracts, editorials, non-peer-reviewed commentaries, book reviews.
  - Papers focusing purely on technical computer science algorithms without marketing/management constructs.

### 2. Formulate Systematic Search Queries
Use Boolean strings across premier databases (Scopus, Web of Science Core Collection, ScienceDirect, Emerald, Wiley, Springer):
- Example Boolean String:
  ```text
  TITLE-ABS-KEY(("artificial intelligence" OR "AI" OR "generative AI" OR "machine learning" OR "chatgpt") 
  AND ("consumer behavior" OR "customer experience" OR "purchase intention" OR "engagement") 
  AND ("marketing" OR "retailing" OR "e-commerce"))
  ```
- Refer to [scopus_wos_search_guide.md](./references/scopus_wos_search_guide.md) for database-specific syntax and export settings.

### 3. Screen and Document with PRISMA 2020
Follow the 4-phase PRISMA workflow:
1. **Identification**: Records identified from database searches + deduplication.
2. **Screening**: Title and abstract screening based on inclusion/exclusion criteria.
3. **Eligibility**: Full-text evaluation for alignment with doctoral scope.
4. **Included**: Final corpus of studies incorporated into the dissertation.
- Record exact figures using [prisma_flow_template.md](./resources/prisma_flow_template.md).

### 4. Build the Literature Synthesis Matrix
Do not read passively. Map every included paper into the standardized matrix:
- **Columns**: Author(s) & Year | Journal & Rank | Theoretical Foundation | Context & Sample ($N$, Industry, Country) | Methodology (Survey, Experiment, PLS-SEM, CB-SEM) | Key Independent/Mediator/Moderator/Dependent Variables | Major Findings | Limitations & Identified Future Research.
- Template available at [literature_matrix_template.md](./resources/literature_matrix_template.md).

### 5. Critical Academic Synthesis (Avoiding the "Annotated Bibliography" Trap)
Never write: *"Author A (2020) found X. Author B (2021) found Y. Author C (2022) found Z."* (Annotated bibliography).
Instead, organize synthetically by **theme, conflicting perspectives, and mechanisms**:
- Group studies around theoretical tensions and boundary conditions:
  > *"While several scholars argue that AI anthropomorphism universally bolsters consumer trust (e.g., Wang et al., 2022; Smith & Brown, 2023), emerging evidence reveals an uncanny valley effect in sensitive service encounters (Kim et al., 2024). This divergence suggests that service criticality functions as an unexamined boundary condition..."*
- Highlight convergence, divergence, and unaddressed intersections.

### 6. Citation & Reference Management
- Enforce **APA 7th edition** across all citations and reference entries.
- Maintain consistent DOIs for all journal articles.
- Flag any missing parenthetical citations or unverified secondary citations.
